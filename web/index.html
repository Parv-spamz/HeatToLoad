RESEARCH SYNTHESIS

Key Grid Takeaways

Empirical findings from the 16-state econometric analysis.

01
Humidity acts as an explosive multiplier. The NOAA Heat Index is the dominant weather driver in SHAP rankings (~272 MW average impact), as sultry coastal pre-monsoon conditions cripple natural human sweating and force sustained air conditioner usage.
02
Nighttime cooling failure drives peak baseload. Minimum daily temperatures (`temperature_2m_min`) rank among top drivers, as hot nights prevent building thermal dissipation, keeping baseline loads elevated through 2:00 AM.
03
+2°C warming consumes existing regional reserve margins. An additional ~5.8 GW of simultaneous peak load requires enhanced dynamic line rating (DLR) and battery storage (BESS) during the 7:00–10:00 PM evening solar fade window.
